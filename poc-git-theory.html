D. One scene, three lenses - not three widgets

LensWhat it answersThe lesson that needs it
folderWhere does this live, and what is literally in it? Anything that introduces a NEW KIND OF FILE in .git: blob, tree, commit, refs/heads/main, HEAD, the index, a packfile
chainWho names whom? Anything about a RELATIONSHIP: tree to blob, commit to parent, ref to commit, "unreachable" meaning nobody points at it, rewriting making copies
